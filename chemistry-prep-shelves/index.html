    Chemistry Prep Shelf¶

    1. Purpose¶

    Cyanotype processing requires a clean, stable work surface for:

    • Mixing sensitizer chemistry (ferric ammonium oxalate + potassium ferricyanide)
    • Measuring and dispensing solutions (graduated cylinders, digital scale)
    • Staging materials (bottles, pH meter, gloves, timer)
    • Post-exposure citric acid wash preparation

    The chemistry shelves provides a counter height place to mix chemistry before appliying. It is folded up for transport and during exposures. Water tap TAP-01, on the pinhole wall left of the shelf, supplies filtered Blue water for mixing and wash-down. Its riser tops at the stowed-shelf height and the spout reaches over the board; ball valve BV-04 on the ¾" branch shuts it off from the prep position.

    2. Location and Spatial Constraints¶

    The shelf is in the widened near walkway, hinged on the pinhole wall left of the battery bank. Deployed, it projects 225 mm into the walkway; folded up, the walkway is clear.

    2.1 Film-Plane Swing and Optical Cone¶

    Film-plane swing. The shelf is down only while mixing, with the film plane parked, and folded flat whenever the plane tilts or swings, so it places no limit on the swing.

    Optical cone. Even deployed, the shelf is clear of the optical cone. Its right edge sits left of the cone's left boundary at the shelf's deepest point (Yd 225):

    cone_left(225) = PH_X + (FP_X_L − PH_X) × 225 / FP_Y
               = 2,454 + (260 − 2,454) × 225 / 2,262
               = 2,454 − 218 = 2,236 mm

    The shelf right edge (X 1,780) is 456 mm left of the cone, so it cannot vignette at any film-plane position.

    2.2 Spatial Constraints¶

    Constraint Value
    Deployed footprint Hinged on the pinhole wall, projects 225 mm
    Stowed (transport) Vertical against the wall, ~25 mm proud
    Work surface height 935 mm above the 140 mm walkway deck
    Walkway (widened) 500 mm deep — ~275 mm pass when deployed, full clear when stowed
    Evap cooler (stow) Slides under the deployed shelf
    Optical cone 456 mm clear

    3. Design Specification¶

    3.1 Shelf Board¶

    Parameter Value
    Width (X) 600 mm
    Depth (Yd, deployed) 225 mm
    Work surface height 935 mm above the walkway deck
    Thickness 18 mm UV-coated white ply — ply-primary (no steel frame)
    Work surface area 600 × 225 = 0.135 m²

    Work surface: 18 mm UV-coated white plywood (Home Depot BPI6WUV2I): resistant to cyanotype solutions and pH 3–4 citric acid, non-absorbent, wipe-clean, and white so the chemistry is easy to see. The plywood is the whole structure (no frame); every attachment lands in a pronged 1/4-20 tee-nut in the ply underside, the project's ply-mount standard.

    Spill lip: a 15 mm HDPE lip closes all four sides: front and both ends at the board edge, and the back lip against the hinge leaf (~38 mm in). HDPE, an offcut of the light-trap stock, is inert in the splash zone.

    3.2 Fold-Down Mechanism¶

    Piano hinge: a 304 SS extra-clearance continuous hinge runs the full 600 mm back edge. The shelf leaf screws (1/4-20 SS) into a row of ply tee-nuts; the wall leaf is tapped 1/4-20 into a 6 mm cleat. The wide hinge leaf puts the tee-nut row 25 mm in from the board edge; a 1 in-open hinge would leave only ~7 mm of ply. The pinhole wall is corrugated, so the cleat and both chain anchors bolt (M8×25) into flat 8 mm steel backing plates welded to the wall crests and tapped M8, giving flat anchors with nothing loose behind the wall. The board swings between horizontal (deployed) and vertical (stowed); the chains slacken as it folds up.

    Stays: two 304 SS chains run from M8 eye bolts above the hinge to a 1/4-20 steel shoulder eye bolt in each front corner. Deployed, they carry the front edge in tension; quick-links at the ends set the length, and with it the level.

    Transport latch: a cam latch at the top holds the folded board against the wall.

    3.3 Load Rating¶

    Design load 25 kg (bottles, cylinders, roller tray, scale), carried by the 2 chain stays (front edge, tension) and the piano hinge (back edge). Computed in chem_shelf_load.py:

    Element Demand Capacity SF
    Board bending (18mm ply, 600 wide, 225mm span, 25kg UDL) 7.3 N·m 324 N·m (assumed 10 MPa) 44
    Board midspan deflection 0.02 mm (L/11930) — —
    Chain stay tension (per chain, 46° from horizontal) 91 N 5,338 N (WLL) 59
    Front-corner eyebolt (1/4-20 shoulder, ~45° off-axis, 30% of in-line rating) 91 N 667 N 7
    Front-corner tee-nut pull-out (1/4-20 4-prong, 18mm ply) 65 N 1,300 N (assumed) 20

    Every element clears with a large margin; the front-corner eyebolts (SF 7) are the governing element. The chain and eyebolt capacities are McMaster ratings. The plywood bending allowance (10 MPa) and the tee-nut pull-out (1,300 N) are conservative assumptions: the shelf carries only a mixing load, and at margins of 44 and 20 a capacity several times lower would still clear.

    3.4 Leveling¶

    Each chain sets its own front corner, so the board is leveled at install by choosing the link each quick-link engages.

    3.5 Fabrication Detail¶

    Sheet 4 gives the board cut and tee-nut positions; Sheet 5 the wall plates, hinge cleat and hinge drilling. The hinge is supplied blank: both leaves are drilled 4× at the same 150 mm pitch (25 mm off the pin line), so hinge, tee-nuts and cleat align, 8 screws in all.

    4. Transport Mode¶

    The shelf folds UP flat against the pinhole wall and latches.

    Check Status
    Walkway clearance Folded up, ~25 mm proud of the wall — full walkway clear ✓
    Film-plane clearance Folded flat — never in the swing envelope ✓
    Overhead clearance Stowed top at Z1,300 — well below the ceiling-corner cable trunking ✓
    Evap stow Evap tucks below the folded board ✓
    Vibration Board latched flat against the wall; no loose span ✓

    5. Operator Access¶

    The operator stands on the widened walkway at the shelf midpoint, facing the wall. The surface, 935 mm above the deck, sits 21 mm above the 36in (914 mm) US kitchen-counter standard (Bob Vila), a comfortable standing height for bench work, and its full depth is in reach; the tap, left of the shelf, fills containers on the board.

    Deployed, the board leaves ~275 mm of the 500 mm walkway: room to work but not to pass. That is acceptable because the shelf is down only while mixing.

    6. Assembly Sequence¶

    1. Cut the 18 mm ply board to 600 × 225; seal the cut edges; fit the 15 mm HDPE spill lip to all four edges (the back lip butts the hinge leaf).
    2. Set the pronged tee-nuts into the ply underside — the hinge screw row along the back edge + one at each front corner.
    3. Weld the 8 mm backing plates to the pinhole-wall crests (behind the hinge cleat + one per chain anchor); tap them M8; bolt the 6 mm hinge cleat on (M8×25 into the tapped plate).
    4. Bolt on the piano hinge: wall leaf to the cleat, shelf leaf into the back-edge tee-nut row (1/4-20 SS screws).
    5. Thread a 1/4-20 steel shoulder eye bolt into each front-corner tee-nut; cap/grind the ~7 mm protruding tip.
    6. Fit an M8 eye bolt into each chain wall anchor ~230 mm above the hinge; hang a chain (quick-links) from each wall eye to the shelf eye.
    7. Deploy; choose the chain link each quick-link engages so the board sits level on a spirit level, then close the quick-links.
    8. Fit the cam latch (1619A74) + keeper; verify the board folds up flat and latches clear of the wall equipment.
    9. Verify: deployed level + rigid under load; folded-up clear of the evap stow and walkway.

    7. Parts List¶

    See the Master Shopping List, filtered to the shelf system for the same parts with suppliers, current prices, and click-to-sort/filter.

    TAP-01 and BV-04 are carried in the water-system BOM; the shelf system adds only the ~1.3 m blue-trunk extension that reaches them.

    8. Maintenance¶

    The shelf has no consumables: the ply surface wipes clean, the hinge, chains and quick-links are stainless (the eyebolts are plain and galvanized steel, acceptable for splash exposure), and the chain links set the level (§3.4). The deploy, stow and inspection steps are in the Operating Manual.
